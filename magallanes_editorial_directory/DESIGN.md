## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial visual identity for showcasing entrepreneurs, craftspeople, and productive ventures across the Magallanes and Chilean Antarctic region. Built under the patronage of regional institutional pillars (Sercotec and INACAP), the interface merges official trustworthiness with the visceral aesthetic of an avant-garde regional lookbook.

The target audience spans local buyers, public procurement evaluators, regional tourists, and international B2B sourcing partners looking for authentic Patagonian enterprises. Rather than functioning as an impersonal e-commerce store with shopping carts and payment gateways, the design system acts as an archival portfolio and direct-contact dispatch channel.

### Aesthetic Movement
- **Editorial Archival & Lookbook Cadence:** Asymmetric grids, dramatic typographic contrasts, high-contrast negative space, and curated metadata stamps.
- **Institutional Rigor with Southern Warmth:** Precision lines, grounded navigation, and tactile warm paper surfaces (`#FAF7F2`) offset against institutional maritime blues and punchy navigational reds.
- **Direct Engagement Architecture:** Streamlined cards engineered to trigger instantaneous contact via messaging, location visits, and direct business inquiries.

## Colors

The chromatic structure balances institutional pedigree with geographic and editorial character:

- **Primary (`#0F69B4`):** Chilean institutional deep azure. Anchors high-priority interactions, primary buttons, structural lines, and authoritative headers.
- **Secondary (`#EB3C46`):** Vibrant civic red. Used selectively for call-to-actions, category indices, live pagination marks, and regional editorial badges.
- **Tertiary (`#103D68`):** Deep oceanic navy. Dedicated to institutional endorsements, executive B2B blocks, dense supplier matrices, and nightfall showcases.
- **Neutral (`#787878`):** Balanced mid-tone stone gray. Drives secondary text, fine dividers, and technical specs.

### Dedicated Tonal Applications
- **Backgrounds:** The primary viewport uses Warm Paper (`#FAF7F2`) to evoke tactile printed catalogues. Elevated cards and modular tiles sit on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Soft highlight zones and filter bands use Ice Azure (`#EAF3FB`).
- **Typography & Structure:** Primary reading copy and high-impact headlines use Dark Editorial Slate (`#20242A`), avoiding sterile digital blacks. Mid-tier grouping borders and subtle caption tags utilize Structural Charcoal (`#58595B`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear tensions between architectural technicality and readable modern utility:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Labels):** Acts as the bold editorial anchor. Its sharp geometric forms and distinctive apertures echo maritime cartography, industrial shipping tags, and contemporary publishing. Headlines must be set tight with negative letter-spacing for impact.
- **Manrope (Body & UI):** Provides a calm, open, and legible counterpoint. Applied to producer bios, business profiles, technical specs, and interface controls.
- **Editorial Embellishments:** Distinctive quotes, origin narratives, and artisan credos may use an expressive serif italic styling (`Fraunces` or a system serif fallback) strictly for decorative one-line pull quotes or artisanal callouts, keeping the main layout firmly rooted in Space Grotesk and Manrope.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout language uses an asymmetrical 12-column editorial grid. It balances rigid structural alignments against deliberate vertical offsets common in print magazines:

- **Canvas Limits:** Max container width capped at `1440px` with fluid scaling. Margin boundaries span `4rem` on desktop, `2rem` on tablet, and `1.25rem` on mobile.
- **Column Allocation:**
  - **Showcase & Featured Profiles:** 7 columns for imagery / 5 columns for narrative copy.
  - **Directory Grid:** Modular 3-column repeat (4 columns per tile) on desktop, shifting to a 2-column staggering on tablet, and single-column layout on mobile.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Large vertical separations (`space-2xl` / 4rem) separate distinct thematic regions (e.g., Craft vs. Agribusiness vs. Antarctic Logistics). Component interior spacing adheres strictly to 4px/8px baselines.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating cloud-like drop shadows in favor of a print-inspired tactile depth model:

- **Flat Border Elevation:** Most containers rest directly on `#FAF7F2` or `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px stroke (`rgba(88, 89, 91, 0.18)` or `#EAF3FB`), relying on chromatic distinction rather than blur.
- **Offset Paper Drop:** When interactive depth is required (such as hovering over an artisan showcase card), use a directional, crisp Patagonian shadow: `0 8px 0px 0px rgba(16, 61, 104, 0.08)`.
- **B2B Deep Layer:** The dedicated B2B module reverses elevation via background immersion: a full-bleed block of `#103D68` containing elevated `#FFFFFF` sub-panels or contrasting `#EAF3FB` tiles, imparting executive authority.

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 1`, shapes lean sharp, disciplined, and functional:

- **Base Radius:** `0.25rem` (4px). Applied to button containers, form fields, filter chips, and image frames.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Limited to overarching modal surfaces and standalone showcase feature blocks.
- **Editorial Stamps:** Outer border stamps remain angular and crisp (0px to 4px) to retain an authentic postal/customs insignia feel, rather than using generic pill rounds.

## Components

### Buttons & Quick Contact
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#0F69B4` fill, white `Space Grotesk` bold text, uppercase with 0.04em tracking. 4px radius. 
- **Direct Connect (WhatsApp / Urgent Ficha):** `#EB3C46` solid fill or heavy 2px outline with icon anchor. Emphasizes swift commercial contact over cart flows.
- **Directory Action:** Clear ghost state with 1.5px border in `#58595B`, text in `#20242A`. Hover fills with `#EAF3FB`.

### Editorial Badges & Certification Stamps
- **"HECHO EN MAGALLANES" / "TALENTO LOCAL":** Small box badges styled with 1px border `#EB3C46`, text `#EB3C46`, all-caps `label-stamp` typography, padding `2px 8px`.
- **Classification Badges (B2B / B2C / Comuna):** Subdued `#EAF3FB` surface with `#0F69B4` text for B2B; neutral stone surfaces for territorial identifiers (e.g., *Punta Arenas*, *Puerto Natales*, *Porvenir*, *Cabo de Hornos*).

### Enterprise & Artisan Cards
- Structured lookbook framing: 4:5 vertical aspect ratio for hero imagery, overlaid with an upper stamp badge and lower editorial card body.
- Body displays: Venture name (`headline-sm`), artisan category, concise bio (`body-sm`), and a persistent bottom action bar containing "Ver Ficha Completa" and "Contactar Directo".

### Direct Filter Chips
- Horizontal sliding list across mobile; structured inline grid on desktop. Active chips receive `#0F69B4` background with pure white typography; inactive chips feature `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#787878` border.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Directory search combines a large `Space Grotesk` input field with inset territory dropdown. Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#58595B`, focusing to a sharp 2px outline in `#0F69B4`.

### B2B Dedicated Hub Section
- Full-width container in Tertiary Navy (`#103D68`). Text inverted to Warm White (`#FAF7F2`). Features high-density institutional metadata tables: volume capabilities, sanitary resolution IDs, and export eligibility statuses.
